What changed

Five upgrades that made the difference

🔤

Typography

Two fonts, clear sizes and comfortable line-height replace mixed system fonts.

🎨

Colour system

A calm green and warm cream palette with strong contrast for text.

📐

Spacing and grid

A consistent 8px scale gives every section room to breathe.

📱

Mobile first

Layout designed for phones first, then enhanced for larger screens.

⚡

Performance

No heavy images or marquee scripts. Pages load in under a second.

🎯

Clear actions

One primary button replaces a wall of competing links.
